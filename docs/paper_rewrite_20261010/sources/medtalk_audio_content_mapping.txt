Consequently, we extract audio content features via wav2vec2.0 and a lightweight mapping network. Our approach achieves perfect lip-sync with fast inference, making it practical for real-time applications. During training, the predicted audio content embeddings, combined with the emotion embeddings generated by the frozen emotion encoder, are used as inputs to the frozen decoder to predict facial expressions.
