# Fixed-clock shape prior: development experiment

The user authorized further optimization after joint_prior_formal30 failed audio timing. This experiment fixes a concrete representation mismatch: the old controller always observed 32 audio frames while 104/128 medoids played only 16 frames. Its pairwise shape descriptors were dominated by expressive mean offsets (92.25% of all-pair squared distances). These observations motivate a different representation; they do not prove its eventual success.

## Fixed design before training

- Reuse the exact 1053 fitting / 199 tuning / 353 development regression membership. The former confirmation353 has been examined and is now explicitly development. Frozen upstream run12 already saw these sources. No new unseen-source or publication-test claim; no sealed test or405 evaluation.
- Keep old face/identity/mouth/global models unchanged. Whole-clip frozen global65 and independent enrollment upper9 provide equilibrium context. Fit a ridge map (alpha10) from standardized global65+anchor9 to clip mean minus enrollment anchor, on1053 only. This supplies a static raw level; it is not a query-motion input.
- Extract native32-frame upper9 windows with hop16, including the first complete window of every valid run. Each teacher window subtracts its training clip observed mean, not its own window mean. Nonzero window offsets retain slow excursions; only training target construction reads the clip mean. Per-channel RMS floors.005, clustering weights equal per source clip; K128 distinct real clip-centered medoids. No time resampling, window recentering or mixed-duration token execution. Fixed-shape distributions and overlap-add may still smooth motion; full-clip metrics, not isolated training loss, determine feasibility.
- Temporal head reads the corresponding32 raw acoustic frames (1540 dimensions, including four prosody features), their native validity mask, and frozen global65. A learned64D frame projection, two small masked temporal convolutions and four temporal bins feed128 categorical logits. There is no GT or generated-motion history input, eliminating that train/deployment mismatch in this design.
- Train a matched static-acoustic head with identical initial weights, architecture, batch order and30epochs. Its input at every frame is the whole-clip observed audio mean. Both heads retain identical global context. A third uniform shape distribution is a diagnostic.
- The single training objective is exact categorical energy score on normalized clip-centered trajectories: sum(p_k distance(y,s_k)) minus half sum(p_k p_j distance(s_k,s_j)). Multiple plausible shapes remain possible; no hard nearest-exemplar label or collection of auxiliary losses. Clip-balanced window weights, AdamW3e-4, decay.01, batch128, gradient norm1, seed20260918; final epoch only.
- At deployment probabilities are read on the same32-frame clock, hop16; the last full window is anchored to the run end if the regular grid misses it. Runs shorter than32 are padded and masked, not stretched; their count is reported as a short-run limitation. Positive triangular overlap-add combines sampled shapes; every draw uses deterministic clip/seed keys. Add predicted equilibrium; primary raw predictions are not clipped, centered or rescaled using target information. Report physical-domain violations and display-clamp amplitude retention explicitly. Missing runs remain separate.
- Use8 fixed draws, all scored. The old RMS normalization is preserved for comparable full-clip metrics. Report raw/centered joint and group fair ES, RMS, variogram, covariance, all-frame speed and acceleration, speaker/emotion strata and low-activity clips. Raw and centered metrics distinguish pose from timing; no seam-copy statistic alone certifies naturalness.

## Selection and success claims

There is no hyperparameter/checkpoint search in this first paired experiment. Calibration199 is a feasibility report; fixed353 is an already-consumed development regression. Evaluate real head, matched trained static head, real-head static/reverse/same-speaker-emotion different-sentence acoustic interventions on shared support. Keep frozen global condition identical under interventions. Bootstrap by sentence,2000 draws.

Timing improvement requires real to beat matched trained static by at least1% centered fair ES with95% sentence CI lower>0, and to beat its own static/reverse/mismatch interventions. Paired support must cover at least70% of temporal clips and at least two distinct sentences; report actual cluster count. RawES, variogram and velocity covariance must not worsen>5% against matched static; brow/eye group gains and speakers are reported separately. Passing one whole-face score alone is insufficient.

Naturalness is assessed separately: all four group RMS ratios in[.65,1.5], all-frame speed and acceleration ratio<=1.5, raw out-of-domain fraction<=1%, display-clamp RMS retention>=.95. Report low-activity amplification and individual speakers even if pooled checks pass. These are development engineering conditions, not publication guarantees. If timing or quality fails, do not replace the default or call audio dynamics solved. A protected full-face diagnostic rendering may still be produced and must be labeled accordingly; copied43 and invalid frames must be exact, and global expression/identity must be visually checked.

All sources, code hashes, weights, fit statistics, predictions, outcomes and failures are retained. Independent recomputation uses saved curves. No adaptive use of353 for this paired design.
